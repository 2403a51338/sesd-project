HOW TO PLAY & TOURNAMENT RULES

Master the mechanics and learn how to defeat all four cyber bot personalities.

✊ ✋ ✌️

Core Game Rules

  • Rock beats Scissors: Crushes the blades.
  • Scissors beats Paper: Slices through sheet.
  • Paper beats Rock: Smothers and covers.
  • Identical Choices = Draw: The turn is replayed without awarding points.
🏆

Tournament System

  • 4 Consecutive Rounds: Quarterfinal, Semifinal, Final, and the Grand Championship Bonus Match.
  • Best of Three: First combatant to win 2 turns wins the match and advances.
  • Single Elimination: Lose a match and your tournament run ends immediately.
🤖

Meet Your Cyber Opponents

Rookie Bot (R1)

Beginner AI. Uses basic random generation with slight rock favoritism. Great for warm-ups.

Smart Bot (R2)

Intermediate AI. Observes your recent choices and uses win-stay / lose-shift heuristics.

Master Bot (R3)

Advanced AI. Uses Markov sequence prediction to counter your next anticipated gesture.

Champion Bot (R4)

Grandmaster AI. Psychological counter-play, streak punishers, and deceptive adaptation.
